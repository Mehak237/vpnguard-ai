THE PROBLEM

The Enterprise VPN Blind Spot

1. Manual & Unscalable Audits

Analysts must open Wireshark and inspect nested ISAKMP proposals packet-by-packet. In an enterprise with 50+ site-to-site tunnels, configuration drift goes unnoticed for months.

2. Silent Downgrade Risks

Legacy branch routers often fall back to 3DES (CVE-2016-2183 Sweet32) or Diffie-Hellman Group 2 (CVE-2015-4000 Logjam). Without continuous auditing, traffic is exposed to nation-state precomputation.

3. Encrypted Payload Opacity

ESP packets are encrypted. Security teams cannot verify whether an active tunnel is truly using authenticated AEAD-GCM or legacy CBC block padding without access to internal gateway configs.

ML Inference on Encrypted ESP Traffic

The Mathematical Signature

CBC Mode (AES-CBC / 3DES): Plaintext is padded so $(L_{data} + 2 + L_{pad}) \equiv 0 \pmod{\text{BlockSize}}$. This forces 100% of encrypted payload sizes into discrete block quantization steps (8 or 16 bytes).

GCM Mode (AES-GCM): Counter mode authenticated encryption (AEAD). It requires zero block-length padding and exhibits continuous payload dispersion.
